**Answer:**
Yes, OpenVINO **2026.3** supports MoE (Mixture of Experts) expert offloading to disk, enabling models like Qwen3-30B-A3B to run on systems with **16 GB of memory** [1][3][4]. This feature is **GPU-specific** and works on **Intel GPUs**, including:
- Intel® Arc™ GPU Series
- Intel® Data Center GPU Flex Series
- Intel® Data Center GPU Max Series
- Intel® Iris® Xe Graphics (and other integrated Intel GPUs listed in [5]).

No explicit GPU model exclusions are stated, but the feature is **not supported on NPUs or CPUs** [1][2]. Sources do not specify whether offloading works on non-Intel GPUs (e.g., NVIDIA/AMD), creating a **gap in documentation**.
